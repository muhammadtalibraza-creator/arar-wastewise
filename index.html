Demo

ARAR WasteWise — demonstration training platform. Fictional demo data only. No patient information, no staff personal information, and no real waste-bag identifiers are stored.

Progress is stored in this browser's localStorage only. It is not a secure central database and not an official training record. A production system would require secure authentication, role-based access, a central database, access logs, backups and a reviewed privacy policy — none of which are implemented here.

This platform complements — and does not replace — the existing waste-bag tracking software, HWMIS, hospital SOPs or authorised operational staff. Training QR codes are separate from real waste-bag identifiers.
